BiteCast Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026 (updated: ads and the Remove ads purchase)

BiteCast is a fishing forecast app. This policy explains what it uses and what happens to it. In short: BiteCast has no accounts and no analytics of its own, it shows ads from Google AdMob unless you buy Remove ads, and your catch log, photos and saved spots stay on your phone.

Location

If you allow it, BiteCast uses your phone's location to show the forecast, nearby water and stocking for where you are. You can instead search for a place by name. Your location is only used on your phone and to request data for that place from the services listed below (they receive the approximate coordinates of the place you're looking at, as any weather or map request does). BiteCast never sends your location to its developer and doesn't track it in the background.

Camera and photos

If you add a photo to a logged catch, BiteCast uses the camera or your photo library only to take or pick that photo. Photos are shrunk and saved inside the app on your phone. They are never uploaded.

Your catch log and spots

Catches, photos, notes and the spots you save are stored only on your phone. If you use Back up or Export, the file goes wherever you choose to send it (for example Google Drive or email) through Android's share sheet. Uninstalling BiteCast deletes this data from your phone.

Notifications

If you turn on stocking alerts, BiteCast schedules reminders on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Ads

The free Android app shows occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and app interactions with ads, as described in Google's ad policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), BiteCast asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. BiteCast never gives AdMob your location, catch log or spots.

Remove ads purchase

Remove ads is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment; BiteCast's developer never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own it, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase after a reinstall.

Services BiteCast uses

To show forecasts and maps, the app requests public data from these services, which receive the place being looked up and the usual technical details of any internet request (such as your IP address):

  • Open-Meteo (weather, marine data and place search)
  • MapTiler (map tiles)
  • OpenStreetMap (place names via Nominatim, and fishing spots via Overpass)
  • U.S. Geological Survey (river and lake gauges)
  • Pennsylvania Fish & Boat Commission (Pennsylvania fishing data)
  • Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Billing (the Remove ads purchase)
  • GitHub Pages (the BiteCast fish stocking data, collected from state wildlife agencies)

Each service's own privacy policy applies to those requests. BiteCast's developer doesn't receive them.

What BiteCast doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics of its own, and no tracking beyond what the AdMob ads SDK does (see Ads above).
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

BiteCast isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
